Help & shortcuts

Add media — click a bin tile to preview it, double-click to append to the timeline, or drag it onto a lane to place it. Drag a Media/Transitions tile onto the MAIN lane (or the ALT lane for an alternate run); drag a Sound onto the SOUND lane. Images drop on MAIN/ALT as 3-second stills. Right-click a bin item to favorite it (1–5 stars) or reveal it in Explorer.

Mic — click the header Mic button, record, trim the take on the mini timeline, then save it into Sounds → Mic Record. Takes are not auto-added; drag the WAV onto a lane.

Freeze — put the playhead over a video clip and click Freeze in the Effects bin. Mid-clip freezes split the clip and ripple every lane right by 3 seconds.

Edit cuts — click to select (Ctrl/Cmd+click for many) • drag to move (drag left of 0:00 into the pre-roll) • drag the edges to trim (snap to keyframes keeps cuts lossless) • click a gap then Del to close it • the MAIN-label button removes all gaps.

Cut tools — with clip(s) selected set Frame (Fill & crop / Whole frame + blur bg), Vol (dB) and Speed in the timeline toolbar. The tiny top strip on a cut shows what was changed.
